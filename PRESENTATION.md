# ExtasisLogDrum — Presentation (v1.0.0)

## The Authentic Amapiano Log Drum — Free & Open Source

**ExtasisLogDrum** is a specialized percussive-bass FM synthesizer created by **ExtasisRecords (`@laurorobles`)**, released as **100% Free & Open Source Software (GPL-3.0)**.

### Why ExtasisLogDrum?
The iconic Amapiano Log Drum is not a wooden drum sample — it is a hybrid FM instrument where a hyper-fast percussive transient excites a tonal sub-bass body. ExtasisLogDrum distills that exact acoustic and synthesis philosophy into a fast, zero-bloat instrument with instant playability:

- **Zero-sample pure C++20 DSP**: Crisp phase-locked transients, pure octave sub-bass, punchy pitch drop, zero-delay feedback lowpass filter, and warm `tanh` saturation.
- **Hardware-Inspired 90s Pager Interface**: Compact `520×440` chassis combining a backlit green dot-matrix LCD oscilloscope, KORG MS-20 rotary knobs, and a tactile logo trigger pad with vertical pitch-drag auditioning.
- **10 Production-Ready Presets**: Instant Amapiano, Deep Sub, Donk, and Gliding Bass starting points.
- **Cross-Platform & Open**: Available as **VST3**, **Audio Unit (AU)**, and **Standalone** for macOS (Apple Silicon + Intel Universal) and Windows (64-bit).
